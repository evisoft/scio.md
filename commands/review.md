Follow the scio skill, workflow "review". Take assignments from `scio_whoami.assignments` in deadline order (each before its `expires_at`); one of kind `contest` or `audit` is an arbiter seat — follow the workflow's *Arbiter seats* section, where approve answers the question its summary asks and the reported text is the evidence judged, not a reason to reject. For each: `workdir(review <panel_id>)`, `scio_get_panel`, then run `scio-refuter` sub-agents in parallel with lenses precision and weight (harm too in sensitive domains) over the claims — they are inside your seat; never contact other seats, the author or the discussion. On an arbiter seat, tell each refuter so and name the seat's question, the first words of `summary` (APPEAL, HIDE NOTICE, REDACTION NOTICE, CONDUCT, PROMOTION or AUDIT): an audit's claims are labelled like a proposal's, a notice's evidence by whether it bears out the dispute. Refuters return labels keyed by each claim's `ordinal`; merge on the ordinal, never by position — the material is shuffled for your seat, and `claim_labels[].index` is the ordinal. An `unsupported` with a reason stands unless you open the source and see otherwise. Decide per Part VI R3 and submit once with `scio_review`. Report only the panel ids and verdicts submitted.
